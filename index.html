Privacy Policy

App: Tracksip  ·  Developer: 2Sunrise  ·  Effective Date: April 20, 2026

2Sunrise ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile app Tracksip ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account we collect your email address and a hashed password. You may optionally provide a display name. We do not require your real name.

Alcohol Consumption Logs

The core function of the App is to let you log drinks. We store the drink entries you create, including type, quantity, date, and any notes you add. This is health-related data and is handled with care.

Subscription & Payment Information

If you subscribe to Tracksip Premium, payment is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We do not receive or store your credit card or payment details. We receive only confirmation of your subscription status from the respective store.

Device & Usage Data

We may collect basic technical information such as device type, operating system version, and app version to diagnose bugs and improve stability. We do not track your location.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Real name (unless you voluntarily provide it as a display name)
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Camera or microphone access
  • Contacts or address book
  • Credit card or full payment details
  • Data from other apps on your device

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To store and display your drink logs across sessions and devices
  • To process and validate your subscription status
  • To send transactional emails (e.g. password reset, account confirmation)
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve the App

We do not use your alcohol data for advertising, profiling, insurance decisions, or employment purposes.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share data only in these limited cases:

  • Apple / Google: Subscription validation through their respective stores
  • Database & hosting providers: Infrastructure we use to run the service, under data processing agreements
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of users

5. Data Security

Passwords are stored using strong one-way hashing (bcrypt). Data is transmitted over HTTPS. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data and drink logs for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all personal data and drink logs are permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days. Subscription records may be retained as required by financial regulations.

7. Age Requirement

Tracksip is intended for users 18 years of age or older. The App involves alcohol content. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us immediately at lorenzo.orozco.garcia@gmail.com and we will delete the account promptly.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Tracksip is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you

  • Export your data in a portable format

Delete Your Account

App: Tracksip  ·  Developer: 2Sunrise

You can delete your Tracksip account at any time. Deleting your account permanently removes your personal data and drink logs from our servers.

Option 1 — Delete from within the App

  1. Open Tracksip on your device.
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon or profile menu).
  3. Tap Account.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm when prompted.

Your account and all drink logs will be deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request via Email

If you cannot access the App, email us at lorenzo.orozco.garcia@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Account" and include the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 7 days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your email address and account credentials — deleted immediately
  • All drink logs and personal notes — deleted immediately
  • Any profile information you provided — deleted immediately

What Is Retained

  • Subscription transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years as required by financial regulations. This data contains no drink logs or personal notes.

Important Notes

  • Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your subscription. Cancel your subscription separately through the App Store or Google Play before deleting your account to avoid future charges.
  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
