Privacy Policy

App: Tracksip  ·  Developer: Lorenzo Orozco  ·  Effective Date: May 14, 2026

Lorenzo Orozco ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile app Tracksip ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Quick summary: We store your account, your drink logs, and your profile so the App can give you a personal alcohol-tracking experience across devices. We use a one-shot coarse location reading only to apply your country's legal BAC limit, and we never store that location. We do not sell your data. We do not use your drink history for advertising or profiling. You can delete your account at any time.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

You can create an account in one of three ways. We collect different identifiers depending on which one you use:

  • Email & password — we store your email address and a securely hashed password (handled by Firebase Authentication, a Google service).
  • Sign in with Apple — we receive a unique Apple-provided identifier and, optionally, the email and name you choose to share. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email" relay, we never see your real email address.
  • Sign in with Google — we receive your Google account email, basic profile name, and a unique Google identifier.

You may optionally provide a display name. We do not require your real name.

Alcohol Consumption & Night Logs

The core function of the App is to let you log drinks and "nights" (drinking events). We store the data you create, which can include:

  • Drink entries — type, alcohol percentage, volume, time, and notes.
  • Night events — start/end time, location label you typed, food eaten, drinks consumed, water intake.
  • Custom drink types you add to the App.
  • Challenges, goals, and progress you create or join.
  • AI Coach reports generated for completed nights (see section 3).

This is health-related data and we handle it with care.

Profile & Body Data (used locally for BAC math)

To estimate your blood alcohol content (BAC) accurately, the App uses information you enter in onboarding and profile settings:

  • Biological sex (used in the Widmark formula)
  • Body weight
  • Country and driver type (general / novice / commercial), used to apply the right legal BAC limit
  • Optional disclaimers and toggles you set

This profile is synced to your account so it stays available across devices.

Location (one-shot, for legal BAC limit)

When you first open the App after signing in, we may ask permission to read your location once, with coarse accuracy ("when in use"). We use the result only to look up the country you are currently in, so we can apply that country's legal drink-drive BAC limit to the warnings the App shows you.

  • The location reading is taken once per session at most, never continuously.
  • The raw coordinate is sent through Apple's on-device reverse-geocoder to obtain a country code, then discarded.
  • We do not store, transmit, or share your GPS coordinates.
  • You can deny the permission — the App still works; you can set your country manually in Profile.
  • You can revoke the permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Tracksip.

Subscription & Payment Information

If you subscribe to Tracksip Premium, payment is processed by Apple (App Store) using StoreKit. We do not receive or store your credit card or payment details. We receive only the receipt confirming your subscription status, used to unlock premium features.

Advertising Identifiers (free tier only)

The free version of the App shows ads served by Google AdMob. Depending on your consent and your iOS App Tracking Transparency choice:

  • If you grant tracking permission, ads may be personalised using your Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA).
  • If you decline tracking, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalised (contextual only).
  • Premium subscribers see no ads at all.

We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain the relevant consents in regions that require it (EU/EEA, UK, others).

Device, Crash & Usage Data

To diagnose bugs and improve stability we collect basic technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, region, and anonymous event analytics through Firebase Analytics. These signals are not used to identify you personally.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Your real name (unless you voluntarily provide it as a display name).
  • Continuous or background location, GPS tracking, or location history.
  • Camera, microphone, photos, contacts, calendar, or address book.
  • Credit card or full payment details (Apple handles payments).
  • Health-record data from Apple Health or any other app on your device.
  • Data about your activity in other apps.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you across devices.
  • To store, sync, and display your drink logs, night events, and profile.
  • To compute your BAC estimate using the Widmark formula on-device.
  • To apply the legal drink-drive limit for your detected or selected country.
  • To generate optional "AI Coach" night-recovery reports. When you request a report, a summary of the night (drinks, timing, sleep, food — without your name or email) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API via our Firebase Cloud Function. The generated text is returned and saved against your account so you can read it later.
  • To process and validate your subscription status.
  • To send transactional emails (e.g. email verification, password reset).
  • To serve ads (free tier only) and measure ad performance, subject to your consent.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve App stability.

We do not use your alcohol data for advertising, profiling, insurance decisions, employment decisions, or sale to third parties.

5. Data Security

Passwords are stored hashed by Firebase Authentication using industry-standard algorithms (we never see plaintext passwords). All traffic between the App and our backends is over HTTPS / TLS. Firestore and Supabase enforce per-user access rules so one account cannot read another's data. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account and drink logs for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account from within the App, we immediately:

  • Delete your profile, drink entries, drink types, night events, challenges, and AI Coach reports from Firestore.
  • Delete your Supabase records.
  • Delete your Firebase Auth user.

Subscription transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years as required by financial regulations. AI Coach API logs at Anthropic are subject to Anthropic's standard retention policy.

Tracksip is intended for users 18 years of age or older. The App involves alcohol-related content. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us at lorenzo.orozco.garcia@gmail.com and we will delete the account promptly.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Delete Your Account

App: Tracksip  ·  Developer: Lorenzo Orozco

You can delete your Tracksip account at any time. Deleting your account permanently removes your personal data, drink logs, night events, profile, challenges, and AI Coach reports from our servers.

Option 1 — Delete from within the App

  1. Open Tracksip on your device.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to Account.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm when prompted. You may be asked to sign in again with Apple or Google — this is an Apple security requirement for account deletion.

Your account, all drink logs, all night events, your profile, and your AI Coach reports are deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request via Email

If you cannot access the App, email us at lorenzo.orozco.garcia@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Account" and include the email address (or Apple/Google identifier) associated with your account. We will process your request within 7 days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your email address, sign-in credentials, and Firebase Auth user — deleted immediately.
  • Your profile (weight, sex, country, settings) — deleted immediately.
  • All drink entries, drink types, night events, and water logs — deleted immediately.
  • All challenges and progress — deleted immediately.
  • All AI Coach reports — deleted immediately.
  • Your Supabase records — deleted immediately.

What Is Retained

  • Subscription transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years as required by financial regulations. This data contains no drink logs or personal notes.
  • Aggregated, anonymous crash and usage statistics that cannot be linked back to you.

Important Notes

  • Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your subscription. Cancel your subscription separately through the App Store (Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions) before deleting your account to avoid future charges.
  • If you signed in with Apple, deleting your Tracksip account does not revoke Apple's Sign in with Apple authorization. You can revoke that separately in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple.
  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
